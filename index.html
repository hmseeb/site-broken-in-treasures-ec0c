In the shop

What a trip to Broken in Treasures gets you

A one-room shop you can actually get through, with someone there to help you find the right thing.

Walk out with a gift for every name on your list

Instead of three stops and a late-night order that may or may not arrive, you browse one shelf-lined shop and handle what you're buying. Shoppers tell us the same thing in their Google reviews — there are gifts here for everyone in the family, not just one hard-to-please person.

Shopping for a birthday, a holiday, a new baby, a thank-you? Tell us who it's for when you call and we'll point you straight at it.

“Gifts for everyone in your family.”

Gina M., 5 stars on Google

  1. 01

    Prices that make this a weekly stop, not a splurge

    Our regulars don't save us for special occasions. Good prices on everything from the small just-because gift to the one you wrap up and hand over — which is why people stop in again the next week to see what's new.

  2. 02

    A real person who helps instead of hovering

    You'll be met by someone who knows the shelves. Describe the person you're buying for, the budget you've got, or the thing you half-remember seeing last month, and you'll get an honest answer — including when we don't have it.

  3. 03

    A clean, calm place to browse at your own pace

    No crowded aisles or fluorescent rush. Shoppers describe the shop as clean, friendly and peaceful, which matters when you're trying to think of what someone would actually like.

  4. 04

    Call first and save yourself the drive

    Our stock changes, so the surest way to know we have what you want is to ask. Call (304) 669-3479 for what's in the shop right now, today's hours, or directions off Main Street — and if you're after several of something, ask us for a price before you come in.
